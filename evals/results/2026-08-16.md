Mechanism (instrumented from `route2-weedle-flee-loop.state`, per-turn button log + RAM): the fight
branch was `up, left, A, down x idx, A, A` then `A x8`, assuming the turn opens on the battle menu
with the move-list cursor on slot 0. Neither held. The trailing A-mash left the next turn anywhere
between the enemy's move text and an already-open move list, and Gen 1 remembers both cursors
(wBattleAndStartSavedMenuItem 0xCC2D, wPlayerMoveListIndex 0xCC2E). Turn 77 opened on the list:
the normalising `up` wrapped Scratch → Growl, `A` picked Growl (PP d02e -2), and every later FIGHT
reopened on Growl while Pidgey chipped 17 → 1 HP; turn 1's `run` opened on "was poisoned!" text,
its d-pad presses were eaten and the A landed on FIGHT → Scratch (PP spent, no escape). The move
list also drops the first d-pad press right after it is drawn (turn 229: `down, down` from Scratch
landed on Growl, not Ember). Fix: before and after every action, B until the "FIGHT" tiles are
drawn (wTileMap 0xC4C2..); read the live cursor (0xCC24/25/26) and walk it one verified step at a
time; PP delta (wBattleMonPP is d02d..d030 — the profile read it one byte early) or HP delta or
battle end = "the move landed"; no A-mash after the move. Decisions are made from the settled state
(wBattleMon is stale on "Wild X appeared!"; a 35/35 lead read 13/35 and fled).

What remains is not menu timing: with every turn landing, `route1-flee-loop` still loses 3 HP per
Pidgey Gust over 3-turn Scratch fights and stops at hp_run_threshold=0.2 (4.6 HP of 23), so it
arrives at 1-5 HP depending on frame-level timing (a 4-frame change in the sync wait gave PASS 928 /
hp 5 / 11 battles; 8 frames gave FAIL 748 / hp 1 / 7). Passing at ≥5 HP needs the run/heal policy
(flee before the projected damage of finishing the fight crosses the floor, or a Center visit), not
the fight branch. `forest-crossing-healthy` is the navigation half (poison white-outs to Pallet, then
the (25,21) pocket); its battles are now the strongest part of the lane.
