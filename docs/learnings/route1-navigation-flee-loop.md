why it worked:  The loop is not a wrong branch, it is a wedged screen. Savestates dumped from the evals/cases/low-hp-wild-battle lane show it: the battle opens on the "Go! CHARMANDER!" text, the run branch's d-pad presses are eaten by it, and its A-mash lands on the battle menu whose remembered cursor is on PKMN — from then on the agent is inside the party screen ("Choose a POKeMON" / SWITCH-STATS-CANCEL / "is already out!") and every fight/run selection just cycles that submenu: no PP is spent, no HP moves, 3 low-HP runs, 10 fight turns landing nothing, run forever (main: 600 turns, 3 encounters). The stall-run cap ALONE does not break it (10 fights / 3 runs / 10 fights ..., still 600 turns on Route 2), because the wedged screen accepts neither. B x8 does: it closes the nested submenu and is a no-op on the top menu, after which the normal selection (which normalises the cursor to FIGHT) lands — verified from five wedged savestates; Qwen 3.8's original recipe (B x8, A x3, wait 240) unwedged the relay lane but the A-mash re-enters the PKMN submenu when fired before a fight turn (4 recoveries, still stuck), so the shipped recovery is B only. Result: low-hp-wild-battle PASS 356 turns / Forest / 4 won (was FAIL 600 / Route 2 / 3). The cap never fires with the watchdog in place, so it is kept as the guard against the provably infinite `run` branch, not as the fix for the observed loop. The health half was a second, unwedged mechanism (fixed on `fix/battle-health-menu-timing`, same day): the fight branch's blind `up, left, A, down x idx, A, A, A x8` assumed the turn opens on the battle menu with the move-list cursor on slot 0, but the previous turn's A-mash left it anywhere between the enemy's text and an open move list, and Gen 1 remembers both cursors (0xCC2D / 0xCC2E) — instrumented on Route 2, the normalising `up` wrapped Scratch → Growl and every later FIGHT reopened on Growl while Pidgey chipped 17 → 1 HP; a low-HP `run` had its d-pad eaten by text and its A landed on FIGHT. Now every action syncs to the drawn battle menu first (B until the FIGHT tiles are in wTileMap), walks the cursor it reads from RAM one verified step at a time, and takes the PP/HP delta as proof the move landed (wBattleMonPP is d02d..d030; the profile read it a byte early): route1-flee-loop lands 25/25 fight turns (was 19/36) and low-hp-wild-battle needs no wedge recovery at all (PASS 194 turns, 0 WEDGE). It still arrives at the Forest gate with 1-5 HP: with every turn landing, that is the fight-first policy taking a Gust per turn down to hp_run_threshold=0.2, a run/heal decision rather than a menu problem (see evals/results/2026-08-16.md, notes).
generalizes:   When a wild battle stalls (no enemy HP progress for WILD_BATTLE_PATIENCE turns), always cap the recovery run attempts and fall back to the highest-damage move; never let a single recovery heuristic consume the entire turn budget.
